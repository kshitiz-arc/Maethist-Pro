This site sets one cookie of its own: none. Everything MAETHIST stores, it stores in your browser's own storage, and the only cookies you can end up with come from YouTube, and only if you open a lecture. The table below lists all of it, because a policy that says "we use cookies to improve your experience" tells you nothing you can check.

Cookies, and the things that are not cookies

A cookie is sent to a server with every request. localStorage and IndexedDB are not: they sit in your browser and nothing reads them except the page itself. MAETHIST uses the second kind for everything, which is why your notebooks can be large and still never leave your device. The law that governs storage on your device — the ePrivacy Directive, and the equivalents elsewhere — covers both, so both are listed.

Nothing here is for advertising

No marketing or advertising cookies are used on this site, and none ever will be while it is free and ad-free. There is no advertising network, no conversion pixel, no remarketing tag, no social login button and no data broker. The list above is not an excerpt; it is the whole of it.

Every browser can delete site data for one site, and every browser has a setting that blocks third-party cookies. Both work here, and neither breaks anything except your saved work, which is stored in the same place. The erase button in the privacy policy does the same job in one click, and lets you export a backup first.
