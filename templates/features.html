Everything it does

Features

Everything below exists for one reason: so you can study from your own material, in one place — no account, no ads, nothing to pay for, and nothing that needs expensive hardware to feel good.

01

Write and draw

Ink

An infinite canvas

Smooth, pressure-aware strokes with five distinct pen feels.

  • Default, fountain, brush
  • Gel and calligraphy
  • Highlighter and eraser
  • Fast undo / redo
Vector

Lines, shapes, selection

Straight lines as segments, rays or infinite lines, with arrow tips and optional named ends. Shapes, plus a selection tool to move and resize anything already on the canvas.

Pages

Notebook mode

Switch from the infinite canvas to clean, page-based A4-style notes, then save the whole thing out as a PDF.

02

Maths that behaves like maths

Compass

Ruler-and-compass geometry

A real compass: set a centre, open it to a radius, sweep an arc. Where two arcs cross, the point stays live — mark it, name it, join a line to it later. Ends, corners and midpoints of everything you draw stay snappable too.

Algebra tiles

Algebra tiles

Drag out unit, x, x² and xy tiles that stay linked to your variables. Label one tile x and every other x tile on the page matches its length.

Shapes

Smart shapes

Circles, triangles and quadrilaterals that know their own measurements — radius, sides, angles, area — and let you type an exact value instead of nudging a handle.

03

Study from your own material

Import

PDF and PowerPoint

Import a textbook, a question paper or a deck and write straight on top of it. Move slide by slide, then export your annotated work back out as a clean PDF or PPTX.

Library

A notebook per subject

Create, rename and switch between as many notebooks as you need — each with its own canvas, pages or imported deck, and a live preview card.

Lectures

Lectures beside your notes

Search and play YouTube lectures in a floating window. Pause on an important explanation and save that moment onto the canvas — double-tap it later to reopen at that exact timestamp.

04

Stay focused, stay in control

Focus

Pomodoro and streaks

A built-in focus timer, study stats and daily streaks. No ads, no clutter, nothing pulling at your attention.

On your device

Your files, your device

Export your whole library as a single .inky file and restore it on any device. No account, no cloud — it is your file, on your machine.

Experimental

AR Inky

On a laptop with a webcam, draw in the air with your finger using hand tracking. An experiment — a glimpse of where MAETHIST could go for live teaching.

All of it is free and runs in your browser. Nothing to install, no account to make.
