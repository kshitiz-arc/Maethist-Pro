Kshitiz Kumar Sinha

Starting is the heaviest rep.

Mathematics educator · Founder of Tungston Theory and HYPERION · Ph.D. scholar in applied ML Mathematics educator Founder, Tungston Theory Building HYPERION

Mathematics, to him, is not a subject. It is a way of seeing.

He teaches CBSE mathematics at Sarala Birla Public School in Ranchi, mentors its IT & Coding Club, runs its Composite Skill Lab, and is writing a Ph.D. in applied machine learning for fluid dynamics at Sarala Birla University. Different rooms, one question: what does it actually take to understand something?

He thinks most of what we call failure in mathematics is really a failure to see. A test records the answer; it rarely sees the reasoning, or the confidence behind a wrong one. So he builds instruments that look further. Tungston Theory makes learning adaptive. HYPERION, which he presented at the CBSE District Level Deliberations on Computational Thinking and AI, measures the distance between doing a procedure and knowing why it works.

MAETHIST is the same idea for the hand: a quiet place where a student can think on paper that never runs out. It is built by someone who stands in front of a class every morning, and every tool has to survive that.

Accessibility

The target

WCAG 2.1 AA

Every page is checked automatically against WCAG 2.1 level A and AA on each test run, and all of them currently pass with no violations — including the editor. Text meets a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, every control can be reached and used from the keyboard with a visible focus ring, and nothing is explained by colour alone.

Touch

Built for a tablet, not a mouse

Controls are sized to a 44px touch target, which is the same floor a screen-magnifier user benefits from. The interface is designed around a ~960px budget Android tablet, so nothing important is hidden behind a hover or a wide-screen-only panel.

Known limits

What does not conform

Drawing on the canvas needs a pointer — a pen, a finger or a mouse. There is no keyboard equivalent for making a stroke, and that is a real gap rather than an oversight. AR Inky needs a camera and a hand it can see. Imported PDF and slide pages are images, so their text is not readable by a screen reader.

Tell us

If something blocks you

If any part of MAETHIST stops you doing something, please open an issue on the repository and say what you were trying to do. A barrier nobody reports is one nobody fixes.
