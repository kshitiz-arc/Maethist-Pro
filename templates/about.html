How it works

On your device

Your work stays on your device

There is no login and no cloud. Notebooks are stored in your own browser, and you can export the whole library as a single file and carry it anywhere. Capture work never leaves your machine.

Free

Free, and staying free

Nothing to upgrade to, no trial that runs out, and no ads paying for it behind your back.

Open source

Open on GitHub

Bugs, ideas and pull requests are all welcome — if something is missing for the way you study or teach, say so.

Accessibility

The target

WCAG 2.1 AA

Every page is checked automatically against WCAG 2.1 level A and AA on each test run, and all of them currently pass with no violations — including the editor. Text meets a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, every control can be reached and used from the keyboard with a visible focus ring, and nothing is explained by colour alone.

Touch

Built for a tablet, not a mouse

Controls are sized to a 44px touch target, which is the same floor a screen-magnifier user benefits from. The interface is designed around a ~960px budget Android tablet, so nothing important is hidden behind a hover or a wide-screen-only panel.

Known limits

What does not conform

Drawing on the canvas needs a pointer — a pen, a finger or a mouse. There is no keyboard equivalent for making a stroke, and that is a real gap rather than an oversight. AR Inky needs a camera and a hand it can see. Imported PDF and slide pages are images, so their text is not readable by a screen reader.

Tell us

If something blocks you

If any part of Inkyscib stops you doing something, please open an issue on the repository and say what you were trying to do. A barrier nobody reports is one nobody fixes.
