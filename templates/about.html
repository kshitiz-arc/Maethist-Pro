How it works

On your device

Your work stays on your device

There is no login and no cloud. Notebooks are stored in your own browser, and you can export the whole library as a single file and carry it anywhere. Capture work never leaves your machine.

Free

Free, and staying free

Nothing to upgrade to, no trial that runs out, and no ads paying for it behind your back.

Open source

Open on GitHub

Bugs, ideas and pull requests are all welcome — if something is missing for the way you study or teach, say so.
